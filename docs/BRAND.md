# Matchlisted brand: logo and colours

Locked 30 September 2026. This is the single reference for the logo, the
colours and the lettering. If something here needs to change, change it here
and in `scripts/build-brand.mjs`, then rebuild. Nowhere else.

## The logo

There are three approved pieces, all generated from one drawing:

1. **The name logo** (primary). The house with the heart inside, beside "Match"
   over "listed.com" in Montserrat Bold. The right leg of the h runs straight
   down into the tall stroke of the d, joining the two words. The dot of ".com"
   is a small open heart. The orange swoosh leaves the big heart, slips under
   the right wall and underlines "listed.com".
2. **The mark**. The house and heart on their own, no wording, with a short
   swoosh.
3. **The strapline logo**. The mark beside "WHERE QUIET SEEKERS MEET HUSH HOMES"
   on three lines of condensed capitals, QUIET and HUSH in blue, the O of HOMES
   replaced by the small open heart.

Fixed decisions (Phil and business partner, 30 Sep 2026):

- House: one line weight, rounded line ends, chimney. The right wall stops short
  of the floor so the swoosh can leave.
- Heart: symmetrical (compass-drawn tops), large and low in the house, open at
  the bottom. The swoosh leaves it in one smooth curve, no corner.
- Name: two lines, "Match" above "listed.com", both the same size. The M stands
  on its own just right of the chimney. The l is its own letter, clear of the
  wall. The foot of "listed" is level with the foot of the right wall.
- The h-to-d join is one straight stroke of constant width.
- ".com" is orange and its dot is the small open heart: no swoosh, a gap at the
  bottom, both ends rounded.
- In the name logo only, the right-hand end of the roof is trimmed slightly so
  it clears the top of the l.
- Tried and rejected: the chimney doubling as the M's stroke, the wall doubling
  as the l, and the dot of the i sitting on the roof line.
- In the strapline logo the small heart is exactly as tall as the capitals.

## Motion

The name logo has an approved animation (`<Logo motion="intro" />` on the site,
keyframes in `src/app/globals.css` under "The name logo"):

1. The house draws itself: roof and chimney, then the walls.
2. The heart is drawn in one stroke and sweeps out as the underline.
3. "Match" drops in from above, "listed" rises off the underline, letter by letter.
4. The h reaches down to the d.
5. The small heart pops in, then ".com".
6. Every five seconds after that, the big heart gives a beat and sends a pulse of
   light along the underline; the small heart beats when it arrives.

The opening lasts about three seconds. `motion="loop"` skips the opening and
runs only step 6. With no `motion` the logo is still. Anyone who has asked their
device to reduce motion always gets the still logo. Do not add other effects
(spins, bounces, colour changes, glows).

## Files

Everything is in `public/brand/`, generated by `npm run build-brand`.

| File | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| `matchlisted-logo-colour.svg` | The name logo, primary. White or very light backgrounds. |
| `matchlisted-logo-reversed.svg` | Dark backgrounds (white house and name, orange heart). |
| `matchlisted-logo-mono.svg` | One-colour print in Anchor (stamps, invoices, fax-grade print). |
| `matchlisted-logo-white.svg` | One-colour white, for use on orange or on photographs. |
| `matchlisted-strapline-block-colour.svg` / `-reversed.svg` | The strapline logo. Campaigns, boards, social posts. |
| `matchlisted-mark-colour.svg` / `-reversed` / `-mono` / `-white` | The house-heart on its own. Avatars, map pins, badges. |
| `matchlisted-app-icon.svg` | The mark on a white rounded square. Favicon and app icon. |
| `png/*.png` | Ready-made PNG exports (transparent) for tools that cannot take SVG. |
| `logo-horizontal.png`, `icon.png` | White-ground copies kept under their old names for older docs and tools. |

SVG is the master format. Send printers and sign makers the SVG.

On the website the logo is drawn live by `src/components/logo.tsx`
(`<Logo />` for the full logo, `<LogoMark />` for the mark) from the same
numbers, via the generated `src/components/logo-paths.ts`.

## On the website

- Colours come from the tokens in `src/app/globals.css`. No hard-coded hex
  values in components.
- Any heart on the site is the brand heart: `<BrandHeart />`
  (`src/components/brand-heart.tsx`), an open line drawing that takes a text
  colour and never a fill. The one exception is the save button on a listing,
  which fills when saved so its state is obvious.
- Links shared on social media and in messages show the strapline logo on
  Ground (`public/brand/png/matchlisted-social-card.png`).
- Browser tab and phone home-screen icons are the app icon
  (`src/app/icon.png`, `src/app/apple-icon.png`).

## Colours

| Name | Role | Hex | RGB | Pantone (coated) | CMYK start point |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Anchor | House, name, headings, text | `#3D4F61` | 61 79 97 | 7545 C | 37 19 0 62 |
| Signal | Heart, swoosh, `.com`, highlights | `#E8693A` | 232 105 58 | 7578 C | 0 55 75 9 |
| Precision | Third colour: strapline emphasis, large fills, decoration | `#3AADDA` | 58 173 218 | 298 C | 73 21 0 15 |
| Ground | Page background | `#F8F8F6` | 248 248 246 | unprinted white stock | 0 0 0 3 |

Notes on the print values:

- The hex values are the definition of the brand colours. They are exact and
  match `src/app/globals.css`.
- The Pantone numbers are the nearest coated matches to those hex values.
  Pantone does not publish its official conversion data freely, so a printer
  should confirm each one against a physical Pantone swatch book before a large
  run. Once confirmed, update this table.
- The CMYK figures are plain screen conversions to give a printer a starting
  point. Ask the printer to match to the Pantone, not to these numbers.
- The website also uses darker steps of Signal (`#B94A1C`) and Precision
  (`#1E8FC4`) where small text needs more contrast. Those are screen-only
  accessibility steps, not extra brand colours. `#1E8FC4` is what colours
  "Quiet Seekers" and "Hush Homes" in the strapline on white.

## Lettering

- Name logo and site headings: Montserrat Bold.
- Strapline lockup only: Roboto Condensed Bold capitals.
- Body text: Inter.

In the logo files the letters are outlines, so no font is needed to open them.

## Using the logo

- Clear space: keep a margin around the logo at least as wide as the chimney is
  tall (about one eighth of the logo's height). Nothing else goes in that margin.
- Smallest sizes: name logo 140 px wide on screen, 35 mm in print. Mark alone
  24 px on screen, 8 mm in print. Below 140 px wide, use the mark alone.
- Backgrounds: colour version on white or Ground. Reversed version on Anchor or
  darker. White version on Signal orange or over photographs.

Do not:

- redraw, trace or regenerate the logo (including with AI image tools)
- change the colours, or make `.com` blue
- stretch, rotate, outline or add shadows
- cut the underline off the name logo, or lengthen it
- separate the h from the d, or re-space the letters
- put the colour version on a busy photograph or a mid-tone background

## Changing anything

1. Edit the geometry or colours in `scripts/build-brand.mjs`.
2. Run `npm run build-brand`. It rewrites every file listed above.
3. Update this document and commit the lot together.

The lettering outlines live in `scripts/brand-glyphs.json`. They were made once
from Montserrat Bold and Roboto Condensed Bold (both Google Fonts, SIL Open Font
Licence). In the name logo "Match" is set slightly tight and "listed.com"
slightly open, which is what lands the d exactly under the h. They only need
remaking if the wording changes.
